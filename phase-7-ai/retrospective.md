# Final Retrospective

Written end of Phase 7, after the capstone gate. ~5 pages.

This is the closing document of the course — a reflection that, years from now, the learner can read and remember.

## Sections to write

### 1. The skills, honestly

For each phase, one paragraph:
- What you can now do.
- What you still find hard.
- One specific example of a moment when something clicked.

### 2. The artifacts

A list of everything you shipped, with links:
- `learning-log` (Phase 0)
- `pomo` (Phase 1)
- `portfolio` (Phase 2)
- `sql-katas` (Phase 2)
- `questly` API (Phase 3)
- `questly-web` (Phase 4)
- The VPS deploy (Phase 5)
- The real product (Phase 6) — name, URL, current user count
- The capstone AI feature (Phase 7) — name, URL, current usage

Take a screenshot of your GitHub contribution graph for the course period. Include it.

### 3. The mistakes worth remembering

Pick 3 specific mistakes from across the course that taught you something. For each:
- What happened.
- Why you made the call you made.
- What you'd do now.

This is the section future-you will read.

### 4. What the course got right and wrong

Be honest. The course will be improved for the next learner using your feedback.

- What was too slow / fast?
- What was unnecessary?
- What did you wish was covered?
- Where did the AI policy help? Where did it hurt?

### 5. Advice to the next learner

A short letter to someone who's about to start Phase 0. What do you wish you'd known?

### 6. What's next

What are you going to build now? What are you going to learn next? What does "good at programming" mean to you now vs when you started?

## Why this matters

You spent 35 weeks on this. The artifacts are real. The skills are real. But the lessons are slippery — they fade if not written down.

This document is the only thing you'll write that's both:
- Detailed enough to be useful later.
- Honest enough to remind you of what you didn't know.

Keep it. Re-read it in a year.
