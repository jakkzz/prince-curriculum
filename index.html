The path

Eight phases, one after the other. Each ends with a gate — a live demonstration with the mentor. You don't move to the next phase until you can do the current one unaided.

How to use this

A 35-week course is long. The structure exists so you can't get lost.

  1. 1

    Daily

    ~3 hours, 6 days/week. Read the lesson, build the mini-project, commit. Every weekday. The streak is the structure.

  2. 2

    Weekly quiz

    End of each week: take the quiz (20–35 questions, 5 sections). Below the passing score? Re-read, retake. No moving on until you understand.

  3. 3

    Friday check-in

    30 min with your mentor. You demo something running. They pick a file at random; you explain it. Done well = ship it.

The hardest part isn't the code. It's sitting down on the Tuesday in February when nobody's watching. Build the habit. The rest is downstream.

Three rules

Non-negotiable.

🚫 No AI in Phases 0–6

No Copilot, Cursor, ChatGPT. Phase 7 unlocks AI — by then you'll have the judgment to know when it's wrong. That judgment is the prize.

📝 Be honest

If you don't understand something, say so. The penalty for confusion is zero. The penalty for pretending is week-12 collapse.

🚀 Ship over polish

Done and ugly beats beautiful and unfinished. Every. Single. Time. Ship first, polish after feedback.

The reward

Earned at Phase 7's capstone gate — your AI feature shipped, real users, eval pass rate, design doc.
