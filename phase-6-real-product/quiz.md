# Phase 6 Quiz

This phase doesn't have a written quiz like the previous ones. The learning is in **doing**, and the evaluation is in the **gate** and **artifacts**.

Instead, the mentor uses this **review conversation** at the end of Week 5. It's a 45-min talk, not a test. The learner should be able to answer all of these fluently.

## Conversation prompts (mentor leads)

### About the users

1. Who are your three users? What do they each use it for?
2. What did the first user say when they tried to sign up?
3. Which user surprised you?
4. If your worst user vanished tomorrow, would your app still matter?

### About the design

5. What's the one thing in your design doc that turned out to be wrong?
6. What's something you cut in week 2 that you're glad you cut?
7. What's something you cut that you now think was a mistake?
8. What did your DESIGN.md not predict?

### About the build

9. Walk me through your hardest week of building. What was hard?
10. What's a bug that took longer than 1 day to find?
11. What's a feature you shipped that nobody used?
12. What's a feature you didn't ship that users keep asking for?

### About operations

13. How many minutes of downtime in the last 4 weeks?
14. What's the most likely thing to break next week?
15. If I rm -rf'd your VPS right now, how long until you have a working app again?
16. What does your error tracking show in the last 24 hours?

### About yourself

17. What's the engineering skill you actually leveled up this phase?
18. What's a skill you thought you'd improve at but didn't?
19. If you started Phase 6 over, what would you do in week 1 differently?
20. Are you proud of this? Be honest.

---

## Gate-day live exercise

The mentor will pick **one** of these tasks. You have 90 minutes.

- **Add a small feature** the user requested.
- **Fix a real bug** in the issue tracker.
- **Add a new endpoint or page** that didn't exist before.

You will be evaluated on:

- Do you start with a plan (or test)?
- Do you know your own codebase?
- Do you ship a PR with a real description and tests?
- Did the change actually deploy to production by the time we wrap?

This is the most realistic exercise in the entire course. It's how the rest of your engineering life will feel.

---

## Pass criteria

- Conversation answers are specific, honest, and reveal real reflection (not "it was good").
- Live exercise: PR opened, tests, deployed, working.
- Gate criteria met (see [../assessments/gate-criteria.md](../assessments/gate-criteria.md)).
